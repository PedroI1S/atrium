## Gotchas

- **Disk:** each worktree's `target/` is 6–9 GB; the Mac has 228 GB. Remove a worktree as soon as its branch merges, and its `target/` as soon as its worker is done. Archive `.orchestration/` first (log 2026-11-15 #1's mistake).
- **agy 401 UNAUTHENTICATED** mid-run can mean the Google login expired; re-login, then relaunch.
- **A background job started with `&` inside a non-background shell dies with that shell**; launch workers with the tool's own background mode.
- macOS has no `timeout`: use `perl -e 'alarm N; exec @ARGV' <cmd>`.

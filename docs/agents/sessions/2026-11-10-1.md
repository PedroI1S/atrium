## What the trial showed about reviews

Worth knowing before the next Flash review:
- A general review prompt on about 3,300 lines came back "approve, no findings". The log showed real work (14 minutes, tests run), but nothing was found.
- Prompts that named the rules and asked for an exact input for each claim found four real defects over two rounds, and the reviewer's inputs became tests.
- The author's own adversarial checks mattered as much: timing one-megabyte hostile inputs found the two quadratic scanners, and a 400,000-input fuzz run was repeated after every change.

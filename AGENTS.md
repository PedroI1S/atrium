# Atrium: instructions for agents

If you were dispatched as a **worker** on a task:
- Follow your brief in `docs/agents/briefs/<task-id>.md` and the worker rules in `docs/agents/README.md`.
- Work only in your worktree and in your brief's owned paths.
- Never change contracts.
- Never commit to `main`/`dev`, merge or push.
- Always finish by writing `docs/agents/handoffs/<task-id>.md`.

Orchestrator sessions: see `CLAUDE.md`.
